# AI Resource Hub

Curated resources for learning about AI, data science, and related engineering practices. Each resource appears once. Labels show the format and an approximate level; 📚 marks a recommended starting point.

## Machine Learning and Data

- **[Practical Deep Learning for Coders](https://course.fast.ai/)** · Course · Beginner to intermediate 📚. A free, code-first course on training and deploying models, supported by the interactive [fastbook](https://github.com/fastai/fastbook).
- **[Supervised Machine Learning for Science](https://ml-science-book.com/)** · Book · Intermediate 📚. An online book about using supervised learning in scientific research, from problem formulation through evaluation.
- **[Essentials of Metaheuristics](https://people.cs.gmu.edu/~sean/book/metaheuristics/)** · Book · Intermediate. Open lecture notes covering stochastic optimisation methods such as genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, and particle swarm optimisation.
- **[What are embeddings?](https://vickiboykis.com/what_are_embeddings/)** · Article · Beginner to intermediate 📚. A detailed introduction to vector representations, how they are created, and where they are useful.
- **[Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/)** · Data and research · All levels 📚. Research, data, and interactive charts on health, population, technology, inequality, and other global issues.

## LLM and AI Engineering

- **[The GenAI Guidebook](https://ravinkumar.com/GenAiGuidebook/book_intro.html)** · Book · Beginner to intermediate. Guidance on the technical and product decisions involved in building generative AI systems.
- **[Ollama in Action](https://leanpub.com/ollama)** · Book · Beginner to intermediate. Practical Python examples for running models locally with Ollama and building applications around them.
- **[What We Have Learned From a Year of Building with LLMs](https://applied-llms.org/)** · Article · Intermediate 📚. Tactical, operational, and strategic lessons from practitioners who have shipped LLM products.
- **[Large Language Model Agents](https://rdi.berkeley.edu/llm-agents/f24)** · Course · Intermediate to advanced 📚. Berkeley's Fall 2024 course on agent foundations, capabilities, evaluation, and infrastructure.
- **[Building Effective Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)** · Article · Intermediate to advanced 📚. Anthropic's distinction between workflows and agents, with patterns for implementing both.
- **[Every Way to Get Structured Output From LLMs](https://boundaryml.com/blog/structured-output-from-llms)** · Article · Intermediate. A survey of prompting, constrained generation, function calling, parsing, and validation approaches.
- **[Your AI Product Needs Evals](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals/)** · Article · Intermediate 📚. A practical process for creating domain-specific evaluations from real product failures and expert judgement.
- **[Data Flywheels for LLM Applications](https://www.sh-reya.com/blog/ai-engineering-flywheel/)** · Article · Intermediate. Shows how logging, evaluation, and feedback can support systematic product improvement.
- **[Building AI Applications for Data Scientists and Software Engineers](https://maven.com/hugo-stefan/building-ai-apps-ds-and-swe-from-first-principles)** · Course archive · Intermediate. An outline of a production-focused course covering deployment, evaluation, debugging, retrieval, and agents.
- **[MLOps vs. DevOps: Why Data Makes It Different](https://venturebeat.com/ai/mlops-vs-devops-why-data-makes-it-different/)** · Article · Beginner. A concise explanation of how changing data and model behaviour add concerns beyond conventional software delivery.
- **[The 2025 AI Engineering Reading List](https://www.latent.space/p/2025-papers)** · Reading list · Intermediate. A dated snapshot of influential papers and projects across models, prompting, retrieval, agents, evaluation, and code generation.

## Research and Continuing Learning

- **[AI News](https://news.smol.ai/)** · Newsletter · Intermediate. Daily summaries of AI research, releases, and technical discussions.
- **[AI by Hand](https://www.byhand.ai/)** · Newsletter and lessons · Beginner to intermediate. Visual and interactive explanations that build AI concepts step by step.
- **[Emergent Mind](https://www.emergentmind.com/)** · Research index · Intermediate to advanced. Topic pages and summaries for recent computer science research.
- **[GPU MODE](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJgIbYl6C5no72a0NUAPcTA)** · Talks · Intermediate to advanced. Technical lectures on GPU programming, CUDA, Triton, kernels, and model optimisation.
- **[Chaos Computer Club media archive](https://media.ccc.de/)** · Talks · Intermediate to advanced. A large archive covering security, privacy, reverse engineering, hardware, and computing culture.

## Communication and Engineering Culture

- **[Doing presentations](https://russelldavies.typepad.com/planning/2015/11/doing-presentations.html)** · Articles and talks · Beginner. Russell Davies' collection of material about preparing and delivering presentations.
- **[Everything I Know About Life I Learned From PowerPoint](https://www.russelldavies.com/powerpoint)** · Book · Beginner 📚. A visual exploration of presentation software, communication, and organisational culture.
- **[The .CLUB Club](https://dotclub.club/)** · Talks · Intermediate. Short talks about engineering leadership, organisational dynamics, and keeping technical solutions simple.

## People and Their Work

- **[Eric Topol](https://www.scripps.edu/faculty/topol/)** · Medicine and AI. Research and writing on biomedical evidence, digital medicine, and AI in health.
- **[Jeremy Howard](https://jeremy.fast.ai/)** · Deep learning education. Courses, software, and practical research from the co-founder of fast.ai.
- **[Rachel Thomas](https://rachel.fast.ai/)** · AI education and ethics. Writing and teaching about applied deep learning, responsible AI, and technology policy.
- **[Andrej Karpathy](https://karpathy.ai/)** · AI research and education. Technical explanations and learning material on neural networks and language models.
- **[Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/)** · LLM engineering and data tools. Frequent experiments, analysis, and open source work on language models and data systems.
- **[Melanie Mitchell](https://melaniemitchell.me/)** · AI and complex systems. Research and public writing on abstraction, analogy, reasoning, and how AI capabilities should be assessed.
- **[Maike Osborne](https://robots.ox.ac.uk/~mosb/)** · Probabilistic machine learning. Research on Bayesian optimisation, probabilistic numerics, AI safety, and AI governance; earlier work was published as Michael A. Osborne.
- **[Christoph Molnar's books](https://christophmolnar.com/books)** · Interpretable machine learning. Open and published books on interpretability, SHAP, conformal prediction, and modelling practice.
